# Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) protects your Vexo account with a 6-digit code from an authenticator app. Once it's on, Vexo asks for a code each time you sign in, with your password or with GitHub. 2FA applies to your own account, and any user can turn it on.

You manage 2FA in your account settings: click **My Account** at the bottom of the sidebar, and go to **Security**.

## Turn on two-factor authentication

1. In **Security**, click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, for example Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password. If you can't scan it, enter the secret key manually. Click **Next**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from your app, and click **Verify**.
4. Save your recovery codes. Click **Copy all** or **Download .txt**, store the codes somewhere safe, and click **I've saved these codes**.

Finish setup within 10 minutes of step 1. After that, start again.

## Sign in with a code

After you enter your password or sign in with GitHub, Vexo asks for a code. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app in **Authentication Code**, and click **Verify**.

- Each code works only once.
- You have 5 minutes and 5 attempts to enter the code. After that, sign in again.
- After 15 failed codes in 30 minutes, Vexo stops accepting sign-ins for your account for a while.

Apps that sign you in through the Vexo sign-in page, such as an [MCP client](/settings/mcp-server), also ask for the code. [Personal access tokens](/settings/api-tokens) don't.

## Use a recovery code

If you don't have your authenticator app, click **Use a recovery code instead**, enter one of your recovery codes in **Recovery Code**, and click **Verify**.

- You get 8 recovery codes, and each one works only once.
- A code is 8 letters and digits. Enter it without a dash, even though the field's placeholder shows one.

To replace your recovery codes, click **Regenerate Recovery Codes**, enter a code from your authenticator app or your password, and click **Regenerate**. Your old codes stop working.

## Turn off two-factor authentication

1. In **Security**, click **Disable 2FA**.
2. Enter your password, or a code from your authenticator app. If you sign in only with GitHub, enter a code.
3. Click **Disable 2FA**.

Vexo signs you out of every session, and you sign in again. Turning off 2FA doesn't revoke your personal access tokens.
