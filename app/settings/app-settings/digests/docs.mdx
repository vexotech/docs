# Email digests

An email digest is a scheduled summary of an app's key metrics and top errors. Vexo sends it daily or weekly to the app owner or to the recipients that you choose.

## Set up a digest

You need write access to the app. Each app has one digest.

1. Open the app's **App Settings**, and go to **Anomaly alerts & digests**.
2. In **Email digest**, turn on **Send this digest**.
3. In **Cadence**, select **Daily** or **Weekly**.
4. Optional: in **Recipients**, enter email addresses separated by commas. If you leave it empty, the digest goes to the app owner. Recipients don't need to be on the app's team.
5. Click **Save**.

To stop the digest, turn off **Send this digest** and click **Save**.

## When Vexo sends it

Vexo sends digests once a day at 13:00 UTC. You can't change the time or time zone. The first digest goes out at the first 13:00 UTC run after you turn it on.

- **Daily**: every day.
- **Weekly**: every 7 days, counted from the first digest. A weekly digest doesn't land on a fixed weekday.

## What a digest contains

The subject line is *App name — your daily Vexo digest*, or *your weekly Vexo digest*. The email contains:

- **Key metrics**: **Events**, **Active users**, **Crash-free sessions**, and **Errors**, each with its change since the previous period. Green shows a change for the better and red a change for the worse. A daily digest compares yesterday with the day before. A weekly digest compares the last 7 days with the 7 days before them. Periods end at 00:00 UTC on the day that the digest is sent.
- **Top errors**: up to 5 of the app's most frequent errors, with their counts.

Errors and crash-free sessions come from your mobile app.

To be told as soon as a metric changes, instead of waiting for the digest, turn on alerts in the app's **App Settings**, under **Alerts** and **Anomaly alerts & digests**.
