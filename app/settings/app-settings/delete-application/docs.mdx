# Delete Application

Deleting an app removes it from your account and stops Vexo from collecting its data. Only the app's owner can delete it, from the app's **Settings** page, under **Danger Zone**. You can't undo it.

When you delete an app:

- Its API key stops working, so Vexo rejects anything that installed copies of your app still send.
- The app and its data no longer appear in your dashboard.
- Its stored data isn't erased. To erase a person's data, or to ask for a deleted app's data to be erased, see [Data Retention](/settings/data-retention).
