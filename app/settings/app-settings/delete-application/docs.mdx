# Delete Application

Deleting an app removes it from your account and stops Vexo from collecting its data. Only the app's owner can delete it, from the app's **Settings** page, under **Danger Zone**. You can't undo it yourself.

When you delete an app:

- Its API key stops working, so Vexo rejects anything that installed copies of your app still send.
- The app and its data no longer appear in your dashboard.
- Vexo keeps its data for 14 days, so that you can [contact us](/contact-vexo) to restore the app if you deleted it by mistake, and then deletes it within 30 days of the deletion. See [Delete an app](/settings/data-retention#delete-an-app).
