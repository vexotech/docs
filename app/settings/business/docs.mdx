# Business plan

The Business plan adds single sign-on, custom roles, a team audit log, and an uptime commitment to your paid plan. It costs **$299 a month**, or **$249 a month billed annually** ($2,988 a year). Usage, such as events, session replays, and extra seats, stays billed on your plan at the [usual rates](/settings/subscription#rates).

### What's included

- **Single sign-on (SSO)** with SAML or OIDC, and the option to require it for your team.
- **Custom roles and per-feature permissions.**
- **Team audit log.**
- **99.5% uptime SLA**: see the [Business uptime SLA](https://www.vexo.co/sla) for how Vexo measures uptime and how to claim a service credit.

Requiring two-factor authentication for your team is available on every plan, including Business. SIEM export of the audit log comes with [Enterprise](#enterprise).

### Upgrade to Business

Business needs a paid plan, because your usage is billed on it. If your account is on the free plan, [enable billing](/settings/subscription#enabling-billing) first.

1. Log in to your Vexo account.
2. Click your profile name in the top-right corner and open **Settings**.
3. Under **Billing**, find **Business plan**, and choose **Monthly** or **Annually**.
4. Click **Upgrade to Business**, and complete checkout.

Business turns on as soon as Stripe confirms the payment, usually within a minute. Refresh **Settings** to see it.

Business is a separate subscription from your plan, billed in advance on its own invoice. Your usage stays on your plan's monthly invoice, so with monthly Business you get two invoices a month.

### Switch between monthly and annual billing

1. In **Settings**, under **Billing**, click **Manage billing** to open the Stripe billing portal.
2. Update the **Vexo Business** subscription to the other billing period.

The portal shows what you'll pay before you confirm.

### Cancel Business

1. In **Settings**, under **Billing**, click **Manage billing** to open the Stripe billing portal.
2. Cancel the **Vexo Business** subscription.

Your plan and Business are separate subscriptions: cancelling Business doesn't cancel your plan, and cancelling your plan doesn't cancel Business. To leave Vexo's paid plans entirely, cancel both.

### What happens when Business ends

- Business stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. You aren't charged again.
- When that period ends, the Business features turn off.
- If your team requires SSO, the requirement stops applying, so no member is locked out. Members sign in with the other methods on their accounts, such as their password.
- Your plan, your usage billing, and your data don't change.

### If a payment fails

If a Business payment fails, Stripe retries it, and Business stays on while it does. To pay sooner, update your card in **Manage billing**. If every retry fails, Stripe ends the subscription and Business turns off.

### Business through an agreement with Vexo

If your account has Business through an agreement with Vexo, **Settings** says so, and there's nothing to manage in the billing portal. To change it, email hello@vexo.co.

### Enterprise

Enterprise includes everything in Business, adds SIEM export of the team audit log, and is sold through a conversation with the Vexo team. To talk about Enterprise, email hello@vexo.co.
