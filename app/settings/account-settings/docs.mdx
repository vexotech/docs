# Account information

Account information describes the high overview information of your profile. This is where your personal information is, and modifiable info as well, such as your e-mail, your profile and other data.

This is where you can change your name or the e-mail that you've provided.

# Billing

The account card shows your current monthly event usage against the free allowance of **500,000 events per month**. Open it to break usage down by event type, including the performance and console events the SDK sends by default. On a pay-as-you-go plan it also estimates the events bill so far and where it's heading by the end of the billing period.

Vexo pricing is usage-based, so there is no plan editor: if you're on the free plan, the **Billing** section's **Add billing** enables pay-as-you-go billing beyond the free allowance. Once billing is enabled, **Manage billing** opens the Stripe billing portal to update your card, view invoices, or cancel. Next to it, a pay-as-you-go account sees the date its plan would end if cancelled, and **Stop collecting data now**, which stops event charges within about a minute (see [Cancelling](/settings/subscription#cancelling)).

See [Subscription](/settings/subscription) for how usage-based pricing works and the full rate table, and [Billing](/settings/billing) for common billing questions.

# Delete Account

Deleting your account deletes every app that you own and closes your account. You can't undo it. If you have a paid plan, cancel it in **Billing** first: you can delete your account once the plan has ended.

When you delete your account:

- Every app that you own is deleted, as described in [Delete Application](/settings/app-settings/delete-application), and its API key stops working.
- You're removed from the teams of apps that other people own.
- Your subscription is removed, and you're signed out.

Vexo deletes your apps' data within 30 days. See [Delete your account](/settings/data-retention#delete-your-account).
