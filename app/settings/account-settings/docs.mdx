# Account information

Account information describes the high overview information of your profile. This is where your personal information is, and modifiable info as well, such as your e-mail, your profile and other data.

This is where you can change your name or the e-mail that you've provided.

# Sign-in methods

You can sign in to Vexo with your email address and password, GitHub, Google, or Microsoft, on every plan. If your team uses single sign-on, see [SSO and Team Security](/settings/team-security#sign-in-with-sso).

## Sign up or sign in with Google or Microsoft

On the sign-in or sign-up page, click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.

To create a new account this way, the provider must confirm your email address:

- **Google**: the address must be verified with Google.
- **Microsoft**: the address must be in a domain that your organization verified with Microsoft, or belong to a personal Microsoft account.

If a Vexo account already uses that email address, Vexo doesn't connect Google or Microsoft to it automatically. Sign in the way you usually do, with your password or GitHub, and then [connect the provider](#connect-or-disconnect-google-or-microsoft).

Vexo recognizes you by your Google or Microsoft account, not by its email address. If your account uses two-factor authentication, Vexo asks for your code after Google or Microsoft signs you in.

## Connect or disconnect Google or Microsoft

In **Settings** > **Security** > **Sign-in methods**, click **Connect Google** or **Connect Microsoft**, and sign in to the provider. To stop using a provider, click **Disconnect Google** or **Disconnect Microsoft**. You can connect one Google account and one Microsoft account. Connecting a different account of the same provider replaces the first one.

An account that you created with Google or Microsoft has no password. Before you disconnect the provider, set a password with **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

Members of a team that [requires SSO](/settings/team-security#require-sso) can't sign in with Google or Microsoft.

# Billing

The account card shows your current monthly event usage against the free allowance of **500,000 events per month**. Open it to break usage down by event type, including the performance and console events the SDK sends by default. On a pay-as-you-go plan it also estimates the events bill so far and where it's heading by the end of the billing period.

Vexo pricing is usage-based, so there is no plan editor: if you're on the free plan, the **Billing** section's **Add billing** enables pay-as-you-go billing beyond the free allowance. Once billing is enabled, **Manage billing** opens the Stripe billing portal to update your card, view invoices, or cancel. Next to it, a pay-as-you-go account sees the date its plan would end if cancelled, and **Stop collecting data now**, which stops event charges within about a minute (see [Cancelling](/settings/subscription#cancelling)).

See [Subscription](/settings/subscription) for how usage-based pricing works and the full rate table, and [Billing](/settings/billing) for common billing questions.

# Delete Account

Deleting your account deletes every app that you own and closes your account. You can't undo it. If you have a paid plan, cancel it in **Billing** first: you can delete your account once the plan has ended.

When you delete your account:

- Every app that you own is deleted, as described in [Delete Application](/settings/app-settings/delete-application), and its API key stops working.
- You're removed from the teams of apps that other people own.
- Your subscription is removed, and you're signed out.

Your apps' stored data isn't erased. To erase a person's data, or to ask for your data to be erased, see [Data Retention](/settings/data-retention).
