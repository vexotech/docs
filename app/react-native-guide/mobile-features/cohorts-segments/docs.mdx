# Cohorts and saved segments

The filter bar at the top of each page has two tools for reusing a group of users:

- A **cohort** is a group of users defined by the [custom events](/react-native-guide/mobile-features/custom-events) that they sent, or didn't send. You apply it to your dashboard's funnels and custom event widgets.
- A **saved segment** is a saved set of filters, such as a date range, country, and app version, that you apply to a page in one click.

Cohorts and segments belong to the app, so everyone on the app's team sees and can use them. They work for mobile apps and websites.

## Cohorts

### Create a cohort

1. In the filter bar, click the cohorts button, labeled **Behavioral cohorts**, and click **New cohort**.
2. Enter a name for the cohort.
3. Choose how to combine the steps: **ALL of** or **ANY of** these steps, or select **in order (sequence)**.
4. For each step, choose **did** or **did not do**, enter the custom event's name exactly, and optionally set how many times, with **≥**.
5. Optional: to match the event's properties, click **+ prop**, and enter a property, an operator, a value, and its type: **String**, **Number**, or **Boolean**.
6. To add another step, click **Add step**. A cohort can have up to 10 steps.
7. Check how many users match in the current date range, and click **Save cohort**.

You can't edit a cohort. To change one, delete it and create it again.

### How Vexo matches users

Vexo works out a cohort's users each time that you apply it, from the events in the date range of the filter bar.

- **did**: the user sent the event at least the number of times that you set.
- **did not do**: the user sent other custom events in the date range, but not this one. Users who sent no custom events don't match.
- **ALL of** matches users who meet every step, and **ANY of** users who meet at least one.
- **in order (sequence)**: the user sent the steps' events in that order. In this mode, every step is **did**, and the counts don't apply.

Property operators compare the event's property with your value: `EQ` (equals), `NOT` (doesn't equal), `LIKE` (contains), `NOT_LIKE` (doesn't contain), and `GT`, `GTE`, `LT`, and `LTE` (greater than, greater than or equal to, less than, and less than or equal to). A step's property filters must all match. Property names can contain letters, digits, `_`, `.`, and `-`.

If the builder doesn't show a user count, check the event names and property names.

### Apply a cohort

Click the cohorts button, and click the cohort's name. The button shows the cohort's name while it's applied. To stop applying it, click **Clear cohort filter**.

A cohort applies only to the [funnels](/react-native-guide/mobile-features/dashboard/funnels) and [custom event widgets](/react-native-guide/mobile-features/dashboard/customize-dashboard) on the dashboard. Other widgets and pages, such as retention, People, and Session Replays, show every user.

To delete a cohort, click the **×** next to its name. Vexo deletes it right away, for the whole team.

## Saved segments

A saved segment stores every filter on the page, including the date range as fixed dates.

1. Set the filters that you want to save.
2. In the filter bar, click the segments button, labeled **Saved segments**, and click **Save current filters**.
3. Enter a name, and confirm.

To apply a segment, click the segments button, and click the segment's name. It replaces the page's current filters. Apply cohorts from the cohorts button, even if they appear in the segments list.

To delete a segment, click the **×** next to its name. Vexo deletes it right away, for the whole team.
