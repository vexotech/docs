# Locations

The **Locations** page shows where your users are, country by country, on a map and in a table. To open it, click **Locations** in the sidebar. Websites have the same page; see [Locations (Web)](/web-guide/web-features/locations).

Vexo finds a device's country from its IP address with the MaxMind GeoLite2 database, so locations are approximate. Devices that Vexo can't place appear as **Unknown**.

## Metrics

Choose a metric with the buttons above the map:

- **Users**: devices that were active in the date range.
- **New users**: devices that first appeared in the date range, counted in the country where they first appeared.
- **Sessions**: sessions in the date range.
- **Avg. session length**: the average length of sessions that have a duration. A country needs at least 20 such sessions, or it shows **Not enough data**. The map colors each country by how its average compares with the overall average, from **Much shorter** to **Much longer**.

Hover over a country to see all four metrics. The table below the map lists every country, with each metric in a column that you can sort. It also shows each country's share of users, and how its average session length compares with the overall average.

## Map and globe

Switch between a flat **Map** and a **Globe**, and click **Reset view** to go back to the starting view. The map needs a browser that supports WebGL2. Without it, the page shows the table only.

## Compare countries

1. Click a country on the map, or its row in the table. Vexo compares it with all other countries.
2. To compare it with one other country instead, hold Shift and click the second country, or click **Compare** in its row.
3. Read the **Metric** table, which shows each country's value and the **Difference**.
4. To stop comparing, click **Clear comparison**.

The comparison is part of the page's URL, so you can share it. When you compare a country with all other countries, the user count of the other countries is approximate, marked **≈**: a device seen in more than one country counts in each.

## Filters

The page uses the date range, OS, and app version filters. It always shows every country, so it doesn't use the country filter.

The dashboard's **Countries** widget lists your top countries by users, and shows a country's cities when you open it. See [Users by Country](/react-native-guide/mobile-features/dashboard/users-by-country).
